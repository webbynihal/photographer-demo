Hello, I'm Elena

Curious about people, light and the in-between.

I started Frame & Soul because the photographs I kept returning to were never the perfectly posed ones. They were the ones with a hand reaching across a table, a laugh that broke the composition, a little bit of wind.

My work is rooted in observation. I want you to feel present while we make photographs together, not perform for a camera.
